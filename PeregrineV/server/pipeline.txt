Core Renderings

1. Forward Rendering; General
> Classic forward shading
> Great for VR, mobile, stylized games
> Supports MSAA
> Simple transparency

2. Deferred Rendering; Cinematic
> G‑buffer based
> Many dynamic lights
> Heavy post‑processing
> High‑end PBR

3. Forward/Clustered Forward; hybrid modern
> many‑light
> transparency

4. Ray‑Tracing; renderphile
> Hardware RT (DXR/VKRT)
> Shadows, reflections, GI, AO
> Hybrid RT + raster

5. Toon/Cel‑Shading
> Quantized lighting
> Hard shadows
> Stylized rim lights
> Outline generation

6. Comic‑Book/Ink
> Hatching
> Cross‑hatching
> Halftone shading
> Thick outlines
> Stylized motion lines
> Color quantization

7. 3D‑Driven 2D Animation
> 3D rigs driving 2D sprites
> 2D layers projected onto 3D meshes
> 2D shading from 3D lighting
> “Paper cutout” rigs
> Pose‑to‑pose interpolation
> Onion‑skinning for animators

8. Pure 2D Sprite/Tile
> Sprite batching
> Tilemaps
> Parallax layers
> 2D lighting

9. 2D‑in‑3D Embedded
> 2D layers rendered in 3D space
> 2D animation planes
> 2D characters in 3D worlds
> 3D lighting on 2D assets
> Camera‑aligned billboards

10. Path‑Tracing; cutscenes
> Offline‑quality rendering
> Film‑grade GI
> Noise‑filtered accumulation

11. Hybrid Accumulation
> Temporal accumulation
> Super‑sampling
> Motion‑blur accumulation
> High‑quality stills

12. VFX/Particle (integrated)
> GPU particles
> Volumetrics
> Fluid sims
> Ribbon trails

13. UI/Vector/SDF
> Signed distance fields
> Vector graphics
> CEF/Chromium compositing
> 2D/3D UI integration

14. XR
> VR/AR
> Single‑pass stereo
> Foveated rendering
> Low‑latency paths
